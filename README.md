## Mirror & Backup Policy

- Each TRTT-owned project should have one clearly identified canonical source repository.
- Organization copies, personal copies, forks, and GitLab backups must be labelled by role.
- Never claim two repositories are synchronized without comparing branch commit IDs and relevant file content.
- Preserve Git history and upstream fork relationships. Do not force-push or overwrite source history as a cleanup shortcut.
- Use feature branches and pull requests. The Owner controls merges, publishing, and production changes.
- Keep tokens and credentials in approved secret stores. Never include secret values in documentation.

## AI & Editorial Disclaimer

AI tools may assist with research, drafting, translation, coding, and review. AI assistance does not replace human judgment or professional advice.

Travel details, prices, schedules, visa rules, availability, and third-party service terms can change. Verify important information with official providers. Affiliate or partner relationships must be disclosed near relevant links.
